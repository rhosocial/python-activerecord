Exclude database-computed generated columns from INSERT and UPDATE payloads. `get_generated_columns()` read `__table_generated_columns__`, an attribute nothing in the tree ever wrote, so it always returned an empty tuple and all six of its call sites were inert -- declaring a generated column via `UseGeneratedColumn` therefore did not remove it from writes, which SQLite rejects outright with `cannot INSERT into generated column`. The two accessors are now split by key space: `get_generated_field_names()` reports field names for the `_prepare_save_data` call sites holding a `model_dump()` result, and `get_generated_columns()` reports column names for the `_insert_internal` call sites holding an already-mapped payload. Both derive from the DDL declaration, so no separate cache is involved, and the split matters because the two views diverge as soon as a field renames its column.
