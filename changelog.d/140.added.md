Added composite index and constraint declarations on the field-level annotation surface: `UseIndex` accepts an authoritative `columns` list whose first entry must be the annotated field's own column, and `UseConstraint` gained the multi-column form for branches with a table-level counterpart (composite UNIQUE / FOREIGN KEY). Added `UsePartitionKey()` for partition keys, extracted onto `__partition_key__` in field order with a `TypeError` when a direct declaration also exists, a `UserWarning` when the key is not contained in the primary key, and a query-time warning plus one CLI stderr line (suppressed by `--quiet`) when a lookup constrains none of the partition columns. Added `uuid_version()` so a model may select time-ordered `uuid7` keys (probed, not imported, since the standard library gained it in 3.14), `natural_key()` so `find_one_or_create` can take its key members from `defaults`, and `reload()` (sync and async) as the other half of the stale-record recipe. Added `.claude/rules/declaration-surfaces.md`, the single statement of what a model may declare on, and rewrote every declarative example in the documentation to the accessor form.
