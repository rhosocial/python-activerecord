Stop dropping `UseColumn` and `UseAdapter` when a derived field is declared with both the `Annotated` form and a class-level assignment. `DerivedFieldHandler._extract` returned at the assignment branch before reading the annotation's metadata, so the SQL alias silently reverted to the field name and the type adapter was not applied, with no warning. Both forms now collect the markers first, making the two declaration styles interchangeable, and the column-name collision check fires for either. Unify the two `ProcedureGraphValidationError` classes: one was defined in `exceptions.py` and exported publicly while the other, raised by the graph runner and resolver, was a sibling class outside that hierarchy -- so `except ProcedureGraphValidationError` against the public export silently missed the raised error. The documented conflict exception in the derived-fields guide is corrected from `ValueError` to `TypeError`, and the two `find_all(derived=True).all()` / `all(derived=True)` examples that would have raised are replaced with the actual API.
