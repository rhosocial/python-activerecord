Move the status families out of core, where they never belonged: `WALInfo`, `ReplicationInfo`, `ArchiveInfo` and `SecurityInfo` go to the PostgreSQL backend, and `InnoDBInfo`, `BinaryLogInfo`, `SlowQueryInfo`, `ReplicationSlaveInfo`, `ReplicationMasterInfo` and `MySQLReplicationInfo` go to the MySQL backend. `ServerOverview` keeps the public slots and becomes a base each backend subclasses (`PostgreSQLServerOverview`, `MySQLServerOverview`), so `get_overview()` keeps its signature while the engine's own fields are typed on the engine's own class. Backend packages must consume the move in the same release cycle; MariaDB writes its own binlog and replication classes rather than borrowing MySQL's. `introspection/status/types.py` sheds 433 lines of other engines' facts, including the paragraph that steered them into `extra`.
