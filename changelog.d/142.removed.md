**Breaking:** removed `supports_partition_info` from core — protocols, both dialect mixins and the SQLite capability mixin; the surviving partition switches are `supports_table_partitioning` and `supports_partition_metadata_introspection` on `PartitionSupport`. Removed `list_partitions` with no compatibility layer: its scheme and bounds now travel together in the `PartitionState` returned by `get_partition_state`. Removed the single `IntrospectionSupport`/`AsyncIntrospectionSupport` names in favour of the four families, with no alias kept. Removed the `TriggerState` dataclass and its export: no method in core or in any backend produced or consumed it, so it was an empty promise in the neutral contract.
